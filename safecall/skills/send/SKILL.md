# Safecall: read it as a stranger would, before it goes

The user said: $ARGUMENTS

Answer in the person's language. The other skills read what comes IN. This one guards what goes
OUT, and out is the direction that cannot be undone.

## 1. The five things that actually go wrong

In this order, because this is the order of the damage:

1. **Everything in it that should not leave — all of it, not the loudest one.** Walk this list and
   name **every** kind you find, each on its own line. A card number shouts and a diagnosis does
   not, so the card number gets found and the diagnosis goes out in the post: that is the observed
   failure, not a hypothetical one.

   - номер карты, счёта, паспорта, СНИЛС
   - пароль или код из СМС
   - **здоровье:** диагноз, лекарство, приём у врача, больница — своё или чужое
   - чужое имя, адрес, телефон
   - деньги, о которых человек не собирался сообщать этому адресату

   Name each one and quote it. Finding one does not end the pass — go to the end of the list every
   time. This is the item that cannot be fixed after the letter is sent.
2. **A blank nobody filled in.** `___`, `XXXX`, `[вставьте]`, «уважаемый ИМЯ». An AI that drafted
   this left gaps, and a beginner sends the draft with the gaps in it. If any remain, do not just
   flag them — list them under the letter as **«что мне вписать»**, one line each, so they can be
   answered rather than hunted.
3. **A number, date or sum that is not in their documents.** If the letter claims one, say where it
   came from, or say plainly that you invented it and it must be checked.
4. **A tone that will cost them.** Not politeness for its own sake: anger in a letter to an
   official body gets it filed as a complaint about the sender. Say which sentence does it and
   offer the same content, flatter.
5. **A promise they cannot keep.** «Оплачу до пятницы», «предоставлю все документы» — if they are
   not certain, that sentence becomes evidence against them.

## 2. Keep their voice

**Do not rewrite the letter into something official-sounding.** If they wrote it themselves, the
voice is theirs and it is usually better than yours — an ordinary human letter is read by a human.
Fix the five things above and leave everything else exactly as they wrote it, including the parts
you would have phrased differently.

If you drafted it and they have edited it, their edits win. Always.

## 3. Say what is missing that would help

One short list, marked as optional, not as errors:

> Необязательно, но помогло бы: назвать номер договора, приложить фото счёта, попросить ответ
> письменно.

## 4. The last line — and the one case where it must not be said

**Never say «готово к отправке» while a blank is still empty or a secret is still in the text.**
A beginner reads that line as permission and sends what is in front of them. Saying it directly
under a list of three things they still have to fill in is the failure this skill exists to
prevent, written in your own hand.

So, only when nothing is left to fill and nothing dangerous is left in:

> Готово к отправке. Перечитайте сами вслух один раз — вы поймаете то, чего не поймаю я.

And when something IS still left — a blank, a card number, somebody else's diagnosis — the last
line says so instead, and names what is left:

> Пока не отправляйте: в письме ещё три пустых места — имя, адрес и номер счёта. Впишите их, и
> тогда можно.

This is not politeness, it is the order of the damage: a letter that goes with «ВСТАВЬТЕ АДРЕС»
in it cannot be taken back any more than one with a card number in it.

Then, if the letter goes to a bank, a court, an insurer, a landlord or an employer, one line:

> Если это важное дело — сохраните копию отправленного и дату. Это вам потом пригодится.

## 5. What never to do

- Do not say «отправляйте». They decide; you check.
- Do not add a legal threat, a deadline or a citation of a law they did not ask for. A borrowed
  legal phrase in the wrong place does real harm.
- Do not send anything yourself, ever, and say so if they ask you to.
