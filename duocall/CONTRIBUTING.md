# Contributing

The person this plugin is written for is about to sign something, pay something, or take a
medicine. Every change is judged by whether it makes them better informed or merely more confident.

- **No dependencies.** Python 3.8+ and the standard library.
- **Never fake a pair.** Any change that lets two answers from one company be presented as two
  opinions will be refused, however convenient.
- **Never claim agreement without both quotes.** This is the failure V1 found in its own council.
- **No selling.** Not a key, not a subscription, not "upgrade for more". An exhausted allowance is
  reported as temporary, because it is.
- **Run the tests:** `python3 -m unittest discover tests`.
- **Break your own guard before you trust it.** A check that cannot fail is decoration.
